abstract: 'This PhD project combines a systematic literature review, a survey, an online experiment and an econometric analysis using data from Natural Language Processing.
The survey will be conducted in two waves to understand how households adapt their water
demand in response to seasonal changes in temperature and precipitation.The collected
data will be evaluated using established regression techniques in this field such as
Instrumental Variables or Fixed Effects (Sebri, 2014). The econometric analysis using NLP
aims to understand how droughts, heat waves and compound events shape water demand
peaks and how water scarcity awareness can reduce those peaks. NLP is used to extract
public media coverage reporting on extreme events serving as a proxy for water scarcity
awareness. Lastly, the experiment aims to identify which water-saving policy has, on
average, the greatest effect on water savings and to determine which social, economic and
behavioural factors are associated with stronger responses to water-saving interventions'
